About SecondBeat

Every instrument deserves a second beat. SecondBeat is a marketplace and community hub for musicians across India.

Our mission

SecondBeat helps artists buy and sell pre-owned musical instruments, find tutors, and connect with other musicians in their city. We built it for artists, by artists — not as a generic classifieds site, but as a platform that understands how gear moves between musicians.

Used Gear

Our first vertical, Used Gear, lets artists browse Instrument Ads and list up to three instruments at a time, with five photos per ad and rupee pricing. Early access is opening soon — join the waitlist to be first in line.

Built for India

SecondBeat is designed for musicians across India. Listings use rupee pricing, local discovery, and categories that match how Indian artists actually buy and sell gear — from acoustic and electric guitars to drums, pianos, and classical instruments.

What's next

Tutors and Community are coming soon. Tutors will help you find or offer music lessons. Community will let you follow artists, discover musicians nearby, and share what you're playing.
