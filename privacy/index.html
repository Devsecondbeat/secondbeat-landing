Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026. This policy explains how SecondBeat handles information when you visit our website or join the early access waitlist.

Who we are

SecondBeat operates secondbeat.in, a marketplace for used musical instruments in India. For privacy questions, contact us at support@secondbeat.in.

Information we collect

When you join the Used Gear early access waitlist, we collect:

  • Your email address (required)
  • Your instrument interest, if you choose to provide it (optional)
  • Technical data needed to prevent abuse, such as a bot-verification token

How we use your information

Your email is used only for SecondBeat communication about Used Gear availability and early access. It will not be used for unrelated marketing or shared with third parties for their own purposes.

If you submit the same email again, we may update your instrument interest and still confirm your signup.

Data storage and security

Waitlist data is stored securely and accessed only for operational purposes related to the SecondBeat platform launch. We take reasonable measures to protect your information from unauthorized access.

Your choices

You can request removal from the waitlist at any time by emailing support@secondbeat.in.

Cookies and analytics

We may use essential cookies and third-party services (such as bot protection) required for the waitlist form to function. We do not sell your personal data.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as SecondBeat grows. Material changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
